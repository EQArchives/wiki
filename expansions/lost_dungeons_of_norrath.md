# Lost Dungeons of Norrath
>This guide is a first draft. Some sections are incomplete and are marked as such.
{.is-info}

*EverQuest: Lost Dungeons of Norrath* (LDoN) is the sixth expansion for EverQuest. It was released on September 9, 2003, as a digital download, with a boxed edition sold at retail.[^4][^5] Players who bought the retail box received an in-game item, a magic bag (see [Retail Box Extras](#retail-box-extras)). This began Sony's practice of offering in-game items as an incentive to buy the retail edition of an expansion.

## Announcement
Sony Online Entertainment announced Lost Dungeons of Norrath in San Diego on June 16, 2003.[^2] SOE described it as a full-sized expansion built around more than forty dungeons, focused mainly on content for a single group. Each group would get its own copy of a dungeon, tailored to the group's strength. Michael Lustenberger, SOE's director of product marketing, said the expansion was "putting the emphasis of the game squarely on the classic fantasy environment: the dungeon."

### Story
The expansion's story picks up at Broken Skull Rock, an area from the previous expansion, The Legacy of Ykesha, as explorers push into the unexplored depths of Norrath's dungeons. The rediscovery of forgotten dungeons, artifacts and power sets Norrath's rival factions against each other. To control the newly found areas, they hire mercenaries and adventurers, and at times pit them against one another. SOE promised hundreds of new quests, with environments suited to players of all levels and play styles.

The dungeons were found by the Wayfarers Brotherhood, a band of adventurers led by a rogue named Morden Rasp. The Wayfarers are also credited with discovering augments.[^4]

### Retail Box Extras
The boxed edition included a small round-fobbed keychain and a claim item, the **Satchel of Abu-Kar**: an 8-slot bag with 25% weight reduction. An in-game quest upgrades it to the **Reinforced Satchel of Abu-Kar**, a 10-slot bag with 50% weight reduction.[^4]

## Overview
Lost Dungeons of Norrath brought widespread instanced dungeons to Norrath. Its content centers on the Wayfarers, an adventuring organization of NPCs who send players on adventures from their camps.

* **Five dungeon themes:** each Wayfarer camp offers adventures in one theme, and each theme has about ten variations of its dungeon zone, 48 in all.
* **No static zones:** every zone in the expansion is instanced and scaled to the average level of the group.[^4]
* **Adventure choice:** players pick the type of adventure and its difficulty, normal or hard.
* **Adventure Points:** a successful adventure awards Adventure Points, which can be spent on items, spells and augments at the Wayfarer camps.
* **Raids:** the camps also offer raid adventures, where multiple groups take on difficult high-end content.

### What's New
* Augments and slotted armor
* Instanced group dungeons at each Wayfarer camp, all with normal and hard modes
* Raid versions of each dungeon theme
* Magus NPCs that teleport players between locations[^4]
* Adventure Points, the game's first alternate currency[^4]
* Specialty merchants who sell items for that currency[^4]
* An adventure statistics window tracking each player's wins and losses, which Bonzz describes as the first and only in-game ranking system[^4]

## Augments
Lost Dungeons of Norrath introduced augments, which add to the power of an existing item when placed in one of its augment slots.

* **Existing items** in the game generally have only one augment slot, so they can take only one augment.
* **Wayfarer merchant items** occasionally have up to three augment slots.

## Adventures by Zone
Each dungeon variation is only offered for certain kinds of adventure. This section collects what is known so far from various sources and is incomplete.

Adventures come in three request types:

* **Boss:** kill a specific named NPC.
* **Kill count:** kill a set number of the dungeon's inhabitants.
* **Collect:** gather a set number of a specific item.

>You will never be offered any of the last five instances you took part in, whether or not you completed them. For example, if you were sent to kill Boss Z in takish_a, you will not be offered that exact adventure again until you have accepted five other adventures. You could still be offered Boss Z in takish_c, or Boss Y in takish_a. Hard and normal appear to count as different instances, so the same adventure may be offered in both modes within five adventures of each other.
{.is-warning}

### Raid Walkthroughs
These summaries describe the main targets and mechanics of each raid.[^4] Raid leaders should adapt them to their group.

#### Deepest Guk
**Ritualist of Hate.** Gukta allies join you as you go: a Warrior of Gukta at the start, then a Master Evoker, several Prophets and a Knight of Gukta. Kill Leklos the Bonekeeper, then The Cavern Creeper to the east, then The Cursed Spore in the north, where the Prophets have it cornered. In the south, killing Gragna the Cursed spawns The Cursed Keeper further in. Killing the Keeper wins the raid.

**The Curse Reborn.** Your allies Scout Granos and Scout Mainil must both survive. Split the raid into three:

* one group stays to protect Scout Granos from attackers;
* one group follows Scout Mainil to the prison area and kills each risen acolyte that Executioner Gimdk spawns, quickly, before it is absorbed;
* one group goes to the northeast room and kills every Energy Focus, Matter Focus and Spirit Focus at the large cauldron to save The First Witness.

Destroying the foci makes Executioner Gimdk killable. The First Witness then attacks; killing him wins the raid.

**The Rescue.** Bitle Cogswin, near the entrance, teleports players to three areas: the crevice room, the aqueducts and the vine room. Split the raid three ways and clear all three areas. Lich Rtrangi then appears to the west; killing him wins the raid.

#### Miragul's Menagerie
**Frozen Nightmare.** Most enemies here see through invisibility, and some see through a rogue's hide and sneak. The first hall triggers the opening fight against Raid Leader Sig Choi and Taskmaster Suitalp; their goblin adds disappear once both are dead. Then hail and follow Durgin Skell, who unlocks each event in turn:

1. Kill the blobs and a sundering sludge. It splits into four severing sludges, and each of those splits into four slippery sludges.
2. Kill four chromatic bonewalkers within ten seconds of each other. Even out their health first.
3. Kill Marrow of the Broken, who has an area attack and spawns single adds.
4. Talk to Sharalla's Warder, then protect Sharalla's Corpse from everything that tries to desecrate it.

Whether or not the corpse survives, the final step spawns Laskuth the Colossus to the north; levitation helps reach him. He can banish players a short distance, reflect spells and use an area attack. Killing him wins the raid, and three chests appear.

**Folly of Miragul's Ambition.** This raid has a three-hour limit, and the raid must keep moving or an Extraplanar Fiend may spawn.

1. Follow Glim until he stops at a hallway. Its enemies can't be killed and return to full health, so don't fight in it.
2. Someone who can drop aggro passes through the hallway, kills a jeweled sentinel and loots at least two Phasing Rubies. Giving them to Glim clears the hallway.
3. Further south is The Synarcana, which has a 2,500-point point-blank area attack. Split the raid north and south:
   * the north group keeps the skeletons dead; they spawn in waves of six for as long as the event runs;
   * the south group fights The Synarcana, which takes no damage while any skeleton is alive (damage shields reportedly still hurt it).
4. Named adds also spawn; each one killed takes 20% off The Synarcana's health.

Killing The Synarcana wins the raid.

**Spider Den.** The goal is to free Researcher Briana. Kill the five Frostfoot lookouts. Each can trigger an ambush of named and trash adds, and you want the named ones: Enchanted Ice Guardian, Swirling Magical Guardian, Archpriest Yakan, Venomous Spider Guardian and Savage Krendis. Once Briana is freed, the raid is won.

#### Mistmoore Catacombs
**Scion Lair of Fury.** Clear the zone and kill four targets: Elite Scion Guardian, Grortrakien the Mutilator, Halusant the Nightblood (southeast room) and Sfisithik the Light Devourer (south room, where Hissing Shadow Lurkers can spawn). Splitting the raid to kill the last two at the same time is optional, but stops the one you aren't fighting from healing to full.

**The Asylum of Invoked Stone.** *Not yet documented.*

**The Lost Party.** A two-group raid to rescue a lost party: Ailill Nye, Bartik Hertigson, Harris Feril, Hilus Greensward, Keelia Fern and Sila Warren. The dungeon is essentially a one-way path.

1. On the way in, kill four mini-bosses: Shambling Archer, Blighted Spiritist, Scion of Blood and Scion of Darkness. One player must loot all four items they drop: Ailill's Phail, Bartik's Heart, Keelia's Heart and Hilus' Journal.
2. In the far northwest round room, stand in the middle with no target and `/say Sila Warren`. A Forceful Presence, which can shadow step, appears with summoned soul adds. Kill them and loot Sila's Medallion.
3. Sila Warren appears. If she doesn't, say `I seek Sila` the same way. Hail her and she follows you.
4. Fight through to Harris Feril and Mayong Mistmoore. Harris flees, and "Mayong" turns out to be Lord Enghearst. Kill him.
5. The player holding the four items talks to Sila Warren for a Warded Bag, then combines the items in it to make a Sealed Warded Bag.
6. That player leads the way to the final room, where killing Harris Feril wins the raid.

This raid was later reused, made harder, as a two-group event for EverQuest's 19th anniversary.

**Struggles within the Progeny.** Part of the Paladin 2.0 epic quest. *Not yet documented.*

#### Rujarkian Hills
**Prison Break.** The goblins here fight in threes; kill each trio quickly, or they run for help (root and snare work on them).

1. Kill three taskmasters, each at a dead end: Taskmaster Devrak (east), Taskmaster Dokorel (west) and Taskmaster Velrek (north).
2. Each taskmaster drops two chests, and one of them, an orcish chest, holds a Heavy Orcish Prison Key. Disarm the chests so the key isn't lost.
3. Take the three keys south and give one each to Eneau Welani, Piyea Frost and Maziyae Xanl\`Utin.
4. Prisoner Guard Talkor spawns west of the prison. Kill him.
5. Free the prisoners in the five southwest rooms, including Jealac Yzinaql, Bacluu Iggn, Gaddian Opaleye and Leannra Nuadr. The fifth prisoner's name is unknown.
6. High Shaman Yenner and Warden Neyremal spawn on a central platform in the southwest. Killing them wins the raid.

**The Hidden Vale.** Bonzz calls this raid "Hidden Vale of Deceit". Crispen Koloff, near the entrance, has lore to share. This is essentially a clear-the-zone raid:

1. Kill four Violent Research Subjects: in the upper northeast room, the middle far east room, the south far east room and the south lower west room. Leave the non-aggressive Steelslave Research Assistant alone for now.
2. Kill three Steelslave Researchers in the far northeast room.
3. In the far middle-west room, kill the Flawless Experimental Brutes and Steelslave Research Assistants, and then the Flawless Experimental Battlelord, who must die last.

Loot is in The Splintering Barrel, The Bloody Barrel and the Researcher's Box of Supplies.

**War March of Imal Ojun.** Fight your way in and kill Captain Grelnik, Captain Kalin and Captain Hurkilin, then Warlord Imal Ojun.

#### Takish-Hiz
**Sunken Library.** Clear your way to Geomancer Paara.

1. Kill the golems: the Sands of Knowledge and the Sands of Life. The Flowkeeper of Knowledge and the Flowkeeper of Life then spawn.
2. Kill the Jeweled Guardians blocking the way to the boss. Up to five can appear: Ridhaz, Wayvia, Bathas, Faetran and Myriana.
3. Kill Geomancer Paara to win the raid.

**The Palace Grounds.** As you enter, a wayward defender, a delinquent defender and an aberrant defender each head off in a different direction. They can't be killed, and they stop moving when enemies are near. Either split the raid three ways and follow one each, or clear the routes to the northwest, northeast and southwest one at a time. Traps along the way can spawn fifteen ravenous insects at once.

When each defender reaches its destination, four waves of five enraged jeweled guards attack. Each location then spawns a boss leashed to that room: Master of the Guard (northwest), Tactician of the Guard (southwest) and Champion of the Guard (northeast). Killing all three wins the raid.

**Within the Compact.** In the round room to the southwest are five targets leashed to the room: Ritana the Convoker, Thosan the Manipulator, Tweyne the Manipulator, Unala the Avenger and Quintessence of Sand. The Quintessence can summon players and stays rooted until Ritana dies. Expect area attacks, and two mesmerizing elementals spawn during the fight. Killing all five wins the raid.

## Progression and the Adventurer's Stone
At the start, players complete a small task that rewards the **Adventurer's Stone**, a charm augment with no stats.

Every completed adventure awards Adventure Points to spend at the camps, and also counts toward your progress in that theme. After enough adventures, NPCs at the camps may have new dialogue that explains the story behind their theme in some detail. Triggering enough of this dialogue adds bonuses to the Adventurer's Stone. Each theme has a set number of these triggers, so you need to complete a certain number of adventures in every camp to give the Adventurer's Stone its full stats. Once fully progressed, the stone can be converted into the **Wayfarer's Brotherhood Emblem**.[^4]

## Merchants
Each camp has Adventure merchants selling augments, spells and armor specific to that theme, along with items available at every camp. Adventure Points can be spent at any camp, but each merchant only sells items up to the number of points you have earned in that camp's theme, revealing more items as you earn more.[^4]

## Achievements
Achievements were added to EverQuest with the Underfoot expansion, and Lost Dungeons of Norrath has 114 of them, worth 2,150 points in total.[^4]

| Category | Achievements | Points | What they cover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General | 3 | 80 | Lost Dungeons of Norrath Explorer (10 points), Dungeon Slayer (30) and Conqueror of the Lost Dungeons of Norrath (40), each for completing five listed achievements |
| Exploration | 53 | 530 | A Traveler achievement for visiting each of the 48 dungeons, and an Explorer achievement for each theme |
| Raids | 5 | 50 | A Conqueror achievement for each theme, for defeating its raids |
| Hunter | 53 | 1,490 | A Hunter achievement for each dungeon (30 points each, for defeating between 3 and 30 listed targets), and one for each theme (10 points each) |

**Tip:** many named NPCs appear in more than one dungeon's Hunter achievement. Killing one in any LDoN dungeon counts for every Hunter achievement that lists it, so you only need to kill each one once.[^4] To get the dungeons you need for Traveler and Hunter achievements, see [Choosing Your Dungeon by Kill Count](#choosing-your-dungeon-by-kill-count).
