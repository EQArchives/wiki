# Lost Dungeons of Norrath
>This guide is a first draft. Some sections are incomplete and are marked as such.
{.is-info}

*EverQuest: Lost Dungeons of Norrath* (LDoN) is the sixth expansion for EverQuest. It was released as a digital download in September 2003. Players who bought the retail box instead received an in-game item, a magic bag. This began Sony's practice of offering in-game items as an incentive to buy the retail edition of an expansion.

## Overview
Lost Dungeons of Norrath brought widespread instanced dungeons to Norrath. Its content centers on the Wayfarers, an adventuring organization of NPCs who send players on adventures from their camps.

* **Five dungeon themes:** each Wayfarer camp offers adventures in one theme, and each theme has about ten variations of its dungeon zone.
* **Adventure choice:** players pick the type of adventure and its difficulty, normal or hard.
* **Adventure Points:** a successful adventure awards Adventure Points, which can be spent on items, spells and augments at the Wayfarer camps.
* **Raids:** the camps also offer raid adventures, where multiple groups take on difficult high-end content.

### What's New

* Augments and slotted armor
* Instanced group dungeons at each Wayfarer camp, all with normal and hard modes
* Raid versions of each dungeon theme

## Augments
Lost Dungeons of Norrath introduced augments, which add to the power of an existing item when placed in one of its augment slots.

* **Existing items** in the game generally have only one augment slot, so they can take only one augment.
* **Wayfarer merchant items** occasionally have up to three augment slots.

## Adventures by Zone
Each dungeon variation is only offered for certain kinds of adventure. This section collects what is known so far from various sources and is incomplete.

Adventures come in three request types:

* **Boss:** kill a specific named NPC.
* **Kill count:** kill a set number of the dungeon's inhabitants.
* **Collect:** gather a set number of a specific item.

>You will never be offered any of the last five instances you took part in, whether or not you completed them. For example, if you were sent to kill Boss Z in takish_a, you will not be offered that exact adventure again until you have accepted five other adventures. You could still be offered Boss Z in takish_c, or Boss Y in takish_a. Hard and normal appear to count as different instances, so the same adventure may be offered in both modes within five adventures of each other.

## Progression and the Adventurer's Stone
At the start, players complete a small task that rewards the **Adventurer's Stone**, an augment with no stats.

Every completed adventure awards Adventure Points to spend at the camps, and also counts toward your progress in that theme. After enough adventures, NPCs at the camps may have new dialogue that explains the story behind their theme in some detail. Triggering enough of this dialogue adds bonuses to the Adventurer's Stone. Each theme has a set number of these triggers, so you need to complete a certain number of adventures in every camp to give the Adventurer's Stone its full stats.

## Merchants
Each camp has an Adventure merchant selling augments, spells and armor specific to that theme, along with items available at every camp. Adventure Points can be spent at any camp, but each merchant only sells items up to the number of points you have earned in that camp's theme.
