## Lost Dungeons of Norrath Lore

### [Rumors on the Wind](/lore/ldon/rumors-on-the-wind)
Chapter 1: Chasing a rumor, lifelong companions Morden and Nedaria embark on a new adventure.

### [Bound by Exile](/lore/ldon/bound-by-exile)
Chapter 2: Tondal Di`Xevar watched from the shadows, his dark elven sight allowing him to spy upon the two explorers.

### [Treachery within the Brood](/lore/ldon/treachery-within-the-brood)
Chapter 3: Morden vows to discover the truth about the rumors coming from Chardok.

### [The Hidden Passage](/lore/ldon/the-hidden-passage)
Chapter 4: A secret council is called in Chardok as Morden and his companions ready for an expedition.

### [Secrets in the Solusek Mines](/lore/ldon/secrets-in-the-solusek-mines)
Chapter 5: Word is received that the kobold tribes are more active than usual and the result could mean great opportunities for the companions.

### [Fires and Frustration](/lore/ldon/fires-and-frustration)
Chapter 6: The searing heat and the Lavastorm Mountains are witness to the birth of a name, the origin of a family.

### [A Shadow Speaks](/lore/ldon/a-shadow-speaks)
Chapter 7: Nedaria struggles with tearful memories because of her "special gift" and entertains a strange shadowed guest.

### [A Dragon's Riches](/lore/ldon/a-dragons-riches)
Chapter 8: Kobolds do not often give in to pride, but lately, one has turned to greed and is looking toward Lord Nagafen's riches.

### [Madman's Curse](/lore/ldon/madmans-curse)
Chapter 9: Fate and dedication leads Calliav to Zebuxoruk.

### [Seeking the Stone](/lore/ldon/seeking-the-stone)
Chapter 10: His mind trapped by chaos, Calliav struggles to find relief.

### [A Path with Purpose](/lore/ldon/a-path-with-purpose)
Chapter 11: Will Calliav find Mordan and thus discover the truth?

### [An Adventurer's Agreement](/lore/ldon/an-adventurers-agreement)
Chapter 12: Calliav will be forever lost if he cannot open his mind to harness control.

### [Search for Lost Lands](/lore/ldon/search-for-lost-lands)
Chapter 13: Visions of strife deep within the earth.
