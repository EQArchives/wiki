# Drakkin
>Drakkin will not become a playable race until The Serpent's Spine expansion, and require game clients that support TSS.
{.is-warning}

## Description
The Drakkin are an artificial race, the youngest of Norrath's playable peoples. Six dragons, calling themselves the Circle of the Crystalwing, defied the elder dragons of the Nest and took captured humans, infusing them with draconic blood to create a race that would protect dragonkind from the threats gathering against it.[^1] Each of the six dragons gave rise to its own line of Drakkin, and a Drakkin's colors, scale markings and horns reflect the dragon that made them.

Drakkin grow up quickly, reaching adulthood within months. As they mature they lose their memories of their human lives and make a pilgrimage to Crescent Reach, the home of their creators.[^1]

Crescent Reach sits in a great crescent-shaped ravine in the Serpent's Spine mountains, west of the Blightfire Moors. The city was carved into the cliff face long ago by Ogres, whose cursed undead descendants, the Nokk, still wander the farms and burial grounds further up the ravine. Just north of the city is the grove where the six dragons of the Circle of the Crystalwing dwell.

As the children of dragons, all Drakkin worship Veeshan. Their faction standing is neutral, so they can travel freely through most of Norrath's cities, though serving Veeshan makes for an unusual mix of friends and enemies among the old-world and Kunark factions.

### Dragon Breath
A line of area-of-effect breath attacks unique to Drakkin. Each is a damage-over-time effect paired with a resist debuff, and the exact effect depends on the Drakkin's heritage. The abilities are earned and upgraded through quests rather than granted at character creation.

### Heritage
A Drakkin's heritage is chosen at character creation, on the appearance screen. Besides determining their look and their Dragon Breath, it gives a bonus to one resist: +10 to Fire, Disease, Magic, Poison or Cold, depending on the heritage, or +2 to all five. Otherwise, Drakkin base resists are the same as Humans'.

### Infravision
Slightly improved night vision, but not as good as the Dark Elf's Ultravision. Has a slightly reddish tint.
